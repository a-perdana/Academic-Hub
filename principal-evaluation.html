Launch hub for the Principal Appraisal

Pick a principal, then open the right record with that principal already selected. What you can open depends on your role in the appraisal (Principal Appraisal: Handbook 26-27 §7, §13).

  1. Primary appraiser (Chief Academic Officer / designated Academic Director) — record the observation visits, write and sign off the annual appraisal, read the 360
  2. Foundation Representative / General Manager — open the signed-off summary (composite, band, recommendation, agreed goals) for principals at your school and co-sign it
  3. Year 1 — a principal in their protected induction year is not appraised
⚠️ The FR/GM never sees item-level scores, observation notes, F3 area scores or individual 360 responses — only the summary. Read the Foundation Rep guide for the yearly rhythm.
