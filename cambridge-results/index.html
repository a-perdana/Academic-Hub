Before you share

  • Share the links. Each school can go straight to its own view: the links below open with that school selected.
  • Check the version. Every file shows the same label at the top (Cambridge Results Pack v1.0 · Results to June 2026 · built 2026-09-24). If two files show different labels, they come from different builds; ask for the latest pack.
  • No student names appear anywhere in the pack. Groups of fewer than 3 students are not shown.
  • These are Cambridge's provisional results, as sent to each school after the exams. Worldwide figures are Cambridge's published statistics for the June series.
  • Shared by Eduversal with its partner schools and their foundations. Please keep the links within your school or foundation.

What was checked

  • The teacher file re-reads Cambridge's raw result files with its own parser; its grade counts match the totals Cambridge prints in each file.
  • 26 Checkpoint averages and 26 partner-school averages are identical in all three files.
  • 25 IGCSE A*–C figures are identical in all three files.
  • None of the 1174 student names in Cambridge's files appears in any of the three files.
  • Worldwide figures were read twice, independently, from Cambridge's published statistics.
