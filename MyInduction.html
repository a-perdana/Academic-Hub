First time on My Induction?
This page follows the induction track you are assigned to. Eduversal runs six tracks — Teacher, Principal, Subject Specialist, School, Academic Coordinator and Exams Officer. On Academic Hub you will usually see Principal Induction (12 months; four windows — Listen, days 1–90 · Diagnose, 91–180 · Act, 181–270 · Anchor, 271–365 — with an external mentor), Academic Coordinator Induction (one academic year with a cross-school guide) or Exams Officer Induction (one examination cycle with a cross-school guide). If you are not in an induction track, you will see "You don't have an active induction" below — that is correct.
