About this page
The appraisal level decides how much of the F2 Class Observation rubric applies to a teacher — 30 items at Post-Induction, 37 at Developing, 44 at Proficient, 45 at Lead (Early Years teachers use the 17-item Early Years instrument at every level). Post-Induction is the first appraisal cycle after the three-semester induction programme; teachers still in induction are not formally appraised.
A level is never assigned automatically and never moves down. It moves up one level at a time, when the appraiser's formal recommendation is on the teacher's finalised annual record in Teacher Appraisal Entry and school management confirms it here, in consultation with Eduversal. A teacher whose level is not set cannot be appraised until you record it. Levels are recorded by your school principal or academic coordinator. A teacher showing not set cannot be appraised until the level is recorded.
