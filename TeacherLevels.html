About this page
The appraisal level decides how much of the F2 Class Observation rubric applies to a teacher — 30 items at Induction, 37 at Developing, 44 at Proficient, 45 at Lead. It is worth setting before an observation, on Teacher Appraisal Entry.
As a school leader you can change the level for your own teachers — the dropdowns write straight to the teacher's profile. A teacher left at not set is assessed as Induction. Levels are set by your school principal or academic coordinator. A teacher showing not set is assessed as Induction.
