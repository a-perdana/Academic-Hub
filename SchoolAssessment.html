KKTP (Kriteria Ketercapaian Tujuan Pembelajaran) is set by your school, not by Eduversal. Enter it to count students below the mastery standard. It is saved in this browser only.

Roll-ups guide where attention goes next.

When students score below your school's KKTP on a Common Assessment, the Gradebook remedial policy applies: re-teach first, then one short reassessment within 10 working days. The KKTP stays the same — the goal is to help students reach it, not to lower the bar.
