Who counts as “the Appraiser”?
  • The school appoints the appraisers who produce the official F2 score. An appraiser must be one of: Subject Leader or Head of Department, Principal, Academic Coordinator or Cambridge Coordinator.
  • The appraiser must hold a superintendent, mid-management (principal) or supervisor certification, and have at least three years of teaching experience at early years, primary or secondary level.
  • A mentor never appraises their own mentee. If a person was ever the teacher’s induction mentor, school management assigns a different qualified appraiser for the cycle.
  • Eduversal Subject Specialist visits are formative only. They never produce or change an F2 score and are not recorded on this page. Informal walkthroughs and Network Peer Review learning walks do not count either.
The appraisal cycle
  1. Start of year Orientation — the Eduversal Appraisal Team briefs school management; each school orients its teachers
  2. Before the visit Pre-visit document review — the appraiser reviews the lesson plan and background; no face-to-face pre-conference
  3. Before & during Administration (F1) — collected by school management, checked by the appraiser
  4. All year Class Observation (F2) — at least 3 formal observations per semester (this page)
  5. Right after Post-appraisal conference — Glow / Grow / Go, immediately after the observation
  6. End of year Teacher’s Work Inspection (F3) — the teacher presents evidence at the End-of-Year Conference
  7. Each semester Student Survey (F4) — students answer at the end of each semester
  8. When complete Publication of final scores — the teacher drafts a goal for next year
  9. End of period Summary report — to the teacher, and to the school at the end of the year
  10. Between cycles Follow-through and performance planning
Source: Teacher Appraisal: Handbook 26-27, section 1 (Appraisal Cycle).
