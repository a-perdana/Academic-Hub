How to read this page
Checkpoint schools are compared on the overall score (0–50) — the standardised Cambridge score, which holds its meaning across series. IGCSE Core and Extended are never pooled: Core is capped at grade C, so a combined figure describes neither cohort. AS and A Level are separate awards, and a candidate who missed a paper is counted as an incomplete entry rather than averaged away. Every one of those rules, and the workbooks behind them, is set out under Data notes.
