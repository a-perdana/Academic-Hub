Principal → Induction Module: Principal 26-27; Academic Coordinator → Induction Module: Academic Coordinators 26-27; Cambridge Coordinator → Curriculum Module: Exams Officer Handbook 26-27. Who uses it?
If your profile carries foundation_representative, school_principal, academic_coordinator, or cambridge_coordinator as a sub-role, your handbook is pinned first. Academic admins see all four without filtering. Sibling handbooks are always readable so cross-functional context is one click away.
The old 90-day "operational ramp" handbooks are retired. Someone newly appointed follows their induction track on My Induction — Principal Induction (12 months, Listen → Diagnose → Act → Anchor, Listen = days 1–90), Academic Coordinator Induction (one academic year) or Exams Officer Induction (one examination cycle).

2

Tick honestly; if a task does not apply this week, mark it N/A — leaving it open for years stops being useful.
3
Open Directory and find your team
See who the other leaders at your school are — FR/GM, Principal, Academic Coordinator, Cambridge Coordinator / Exams Officer, and the Heads of Department and Guidance Coordinator if your school lists them. If your own card has missing fields, ask a central admin to fix it.

Read the most recent Meeting for your school
Fastest way to catch up on what your school's leadership team is working on. Skim the agenda items — especially anything tagged "decision" or "action".
5

✦Personalized for you
For: Foundation Representative
Governance — Eduversal at your school
As Foundation Representative / General Manager (FR/GM), you represent Eduversal at the school and hold the highest school-level governance authority. Your handbook focuses on governance touchpoints, escalation paths and your part in the annual Principal Appraisal.
  • In the Principal Appraisal you are the governance-side co-reviewer and summary co-signer; the Chief Academic Officer / designated Academic Director is the primary appraiser. At year end, open the signed-off summary from Principal Evaluation (Admin dropdown) and co-sign it. You do not see item-level scores or observation notes.
  • You are excluded at rule level from Principal Coaching notes — coaching confidentiality is a Principal Appraisal Framework rule (Handbook 26-27 §10), not an Induction Charter rule.
  • School-wide decisions you take to your team land on Decisions; meetings you chair go to Meetings.
✦Personalized for you
For: School Principal
Operational lead — the academic tone
As Principal, you lead the school day to day, own Teacher Induction in your school, and are one of the school's appraisers. A newly appointed Principal follows Principal Induction — Listen → Diagnose → Act → Anchor windows over 12 months, with Listen = days 1–90. For the running routine, use the School Principals Checklist 26-27.
  • My Induction (under the My Hub dropdown) carries your Principal Induction if you are newly appointed. A Year-1 principal receives no formal appraisal.
  • When a new teacher joins, you assign the colleague and an eligible mentor, and you lead the Stage 4 Integration Checkpoint and the Final Induction Completion Review. The school's live records are kept by the Teacher Induction Coordinator in the central Induction Control Centre; Team Induction + Observation Entry hold only the observation write-ups and pulse view.
  • Skim the Academic Coordinator and Exams Officer guides when their work overlaps yours.
✦Personalized for you
For: Academic Coordinator
Curriculum + assessment delivery
As Academic Coordinator, you are the person who notices drift before it becomes a crisis — curriculum coverage, pacing across all subjects, results analysis, classroom observation and subject counselling. Your year runs on the four EASE Weeks: Weeks 1 and 3 (mid-semester, EASE Growth, lessons continue) and Weeks 2 and 4 (semester-end Synchronized Test, lessons stop).
  • Syllabus Coverage + Curriculum Map are your primary dashboards. Check pacing against plan at each EASE Week, and between them look for classes more than a chapter behind.
  • Teacher Appraisal Entry + Appraisal Calibration are appraisal tools. With the Principal and Subject Leaders you formally observe each teacher in the Teacher Appraisal population at least three times per semester, recorded in Edunav Appraisal.
  • Schools in the Students Hub pilot (Years 7-8) — the School Assessment roll-up gives you a per-class view of EASE Assessment (Common Assessment) Teacher-Paced Test results for your semester results analysis.
✦Personalized for you
For: Cambridge Coordinator
Cambridge programme stewardship
As Cambridge Coordinator you usually also carry the Exams Officer role — the school's administrative link with Cambridge International: entries, access arrangements, secure storage with two or three named key holders, exam days, script despatch, results and your evidence file. The Principal is Head of Centre and keeps final accountability; the Quality Nominee oversees examination standards. If your school separates the two roles, the Coordinator owns governance and strategy and the Exams Officer owns logistics and documentation — agree the split in writing at the start of the year (Curriculum Module: Exams Officer Handbook 26-27 §3).
  • Cambridge Calendar + Cambridge Standards are your reference pages. Entries deadlines are the most time-sensitive part of the role — see the Curriculum Module: Exams Officer Handbook 26-27.
  • Cambridge Exams Dashboard + Pathway Simulator + Cambridge Results give you the data side.
  • You work closely with Eduversal Subject Specialists. A newly appointed Exams Officer follows Induction Module: Exams Officers 26-27 — one full examination cycle with a cross-school guide, ending Certified or Extended.
✦Personalized for you
For: Academic Admin
You see every role guide
You bypass sub-role gating. All four role guides are open to you, the cross-school workspace (Meetings / Decisions / Directory) shows every school, and you have full edit rights wherever a school leader has create permission.
  • If a school leader reports a "locked" page, check the page-access map in CH /page-access — most issues are a slug that does not match a data-nav-key.
  • When you flip a user's sub-role from CH /console, it takes effect on their next sign-in (or after a hard refresh).
  • Use Directory to audit who at each partner school holds which sub-role.

Short, plain meanings for the terms that show up most often across the four role guides.

Foundation Representativesub-role on your AH profile
Foundation Representative / General Manager (FR/GM) — represents Eduversal at the school; the highest school-level governance authority. Co-reviews and co-signs the summary of the Principal's annual appraisal (the Chief Academic Officer / designated Academic Director is the primary appraiser). Excluded at rule level from Principal coaching notes (coaching confidentiality).
School Principalsub-role on your AH profile
Day-to-day lead of the school. Owns Teacher Induction in the school (assigns each new teacher's colleague and mentor, leads the two checkpoints) and is one of the school's appraisers.
Academic Coordinatorsub-role on your AH profile
The person who notices drift before it becomes a crisis — coverage, pacing at each EASE Week, results analysis, classroom observation and subject counselling.
Cambridge Coordinatorsub-role on your AH profile
The Cambridge lead at the school. In many schools also the Exams Officer: entries, access arrangements, secure storage, exam days, script despatch and results, with the Principal as Head of Centre (Curriculum Module: Exams Officer Handbook 26-27). Works closely with Eduversal Subject Specialists.
