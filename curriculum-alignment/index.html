Grades 7–8 · Lower Secondary, Stages 7–9
Maths Science

Cambridge’s suggested teaching time: maths 4–5 h a week (about 120–150 h per stage), science about 3 h a week (90 h per stage). Three stages in two years ≈ 180–225 h of maths and ≈ 135 h of science a year.

Grades 9–10 · IGCSE
Maths Each science

Cambridge guidance: about 130 h per subject for the whole course ≈ 65 h a year.

How to read these figures
  • Reference sources. Cambridge: CUP coursebooks (maths, chemistry, Lower Secondary science) or syllabus sections (IGCSE and AS & A Level biology and physics); every row carries the official syllabus statements it teaches. KM: Buku Siswa, checked against CP 046/2025.
  • Presence, not depth. A topic counts once, whether a book gives it a page or a chapter.
  • Grade pairing follows our timetable: Cambridge Stages 7–9 in Grades 7–8, IGCSE in 9–10, AS & A Level in 11–12.
  • KM hours are lesson time. Kokurikuler project time is shown separately.
  • Book editions. The SMA science books are 2022 editions, written before CP 046/2025.
  • Kelas X IPA is one subject; schools may teach its physics, chemistry and biology content integrated, in blocks or in parallel.
  • Mechanics (9709 Paper 4) is taught in KM Fisika; the maths view compares Matematika books only.

Sources: Kepka BSKAP 046/H/KR/2025 · Permendikdasmen 13/2025, Tabel 6–10 · Cambridge syllabi · CUP coursebooks · Kemendikbud Buku Siswa. CP 046/2025 remains the reference for maths and science; Kepka BKPDM 020/2026 amends only Pendidikan Agama dan Budi Pekerti.
