The record of a scheduled leadership-observation visit across the 8 foci (P1–P8). Code each focus Established, Developing or Not Observed and write the evidence. The visit itself is not scored; the E / D ratings of valid scheduled visits feed F2 of the annual Principal Appraisal.

A leadership-observation visit record

The Chief Academic Officer / designated Academic Director (or an assigned Eduversal observer) records what they saw during a scheduled visit. There are at least two visits a year: Visit 1 in Sept–Oct (paired with an on-site School Appraisal visit where practicable) and Visit 2 in Feb–Apr.

  1. Set the visit — date, visit type, F2 window, and whether the principal was told in advance that it counts
  2. Code the 8 foci — Established, Developing or Not Observed, with the evidence you saw
  3. Submit — the record locks; valid scheduled visits feed F2 of the annual appraisal
⚠️ Keep the two systems separate. A trip may serve School Appraisal too, but the School Appraisal reviews the school and this visit reviews the principal's leadership. The Foundation Representative / General Manager and the yayasan do not see these notes.
Writes: CAO / assigned observer Reads own: Principal, after submission Feeds: F2 (valid visits only)
